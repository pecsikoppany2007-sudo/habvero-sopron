What we stock in the shop

  1. Baking supplies

    For example Decora doughnut moulds, Silikomart silicone moulds, cutters, muffin cases and cake boxes.

  2. Ingredients

    For example Belgian Callebaut chocolate, including ruby and gold, and Fractal Colors gel food colouring.

  3. Cake-decorating supplies

    For example handmade marzipan figures, Cake Masters sprinkles, edible eyes and cake toppers with lettering.

There's no full product list or prices on this site. If you're looking for something specific, you can write to us on our Facebook page.
